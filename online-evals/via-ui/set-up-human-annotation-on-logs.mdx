## The need for human evaluation

While automated evaluators can provide baseline assessments, they may not capture nuanced human judgment, context, and emotional understanding. Human evaluation complements automated evaluation by providing qualitative feedback, detailed comments, and rewritten outputs that help refine your AI applications.

Human evaluators configured for logs are the same evaluators used in test runs, ensuring consistency across your evaluation workflow.

<Note>

**Before you start**

You need to have logging set up to capture interactions between your LLM and users before you can evaluate them. To do so, integrate the [Maxim SDK](/tracing/tracing-via-sdk/traces) into your application.

Also, if you do not have a **Human Evaluator** created in your workspace, create one by navigating to the **Evaluators** (<Icon icon="square-check" className="w-5 h-5 inline" />) tab from the sidebar, as you'll need it to set up human evaluation.

</Note>

## Setting up human evaluation

<Steps>

<Step title="Navigate to repository">

Navigate to the log repository where you want to set up human evaluation.

</Step>

<Step title="Configure evaluation">

Click `Configure evaluation` in the top right corner of the page. This opens the evaluation configuration sheet.

</Step>

<Step title="Select human evaluators">

In the **Human evaluation** section, select the human evaluators you want to use:

- **Session evaluators**: For multi-turn interactions (sessions)

- **Trace evaluators**: For single responses (traces)

Choose evaluators from the dropdown. The same human evaluators used in test runs are available here.

</Step>

<Step title="Save configuration">

Click `Save configurations` at the bottom of the sheet to save your human evaluation setup.

</Step>

</Steps>

Once human evaluators are configured, columns for each evaluator are automatically added to the logs table. Team members can annotate logs directly from these columns.

## Annotating logs

You can annotate logs from two places:

### From the logs table

When human evaluators are configured, columns for each evaluator appear in the logs table:

1. Click on any cell in a human evaluator column

2. In the annotation form, provide a rating for that evaluator

3. Optionally add comments or provide a rewritten output

4. Click `Save` to submit your annotation

The column shows the average score across all team members who have annotated that log.

![Screenshot of the logs table with human evaluator columns](/images/docs/online-evals/via-ui/set-up-human-annotation-on-logs/human-annotation-logs-table.png)

### From trace details

1. Open any trace from the logs table

2. Click the `Annotate` button in the top right corner of the trace details sheet

3. In the annotation form, provide ratings for all configured human evaluators at once

4. Optionally add comments for each evaluator or provide a rewritten output

5. Save your annotations

![Screenshot of the trace details sheet with the annotate button highlighted](/images/docs/online-evals/via-ui/set-up-human-annotation-on-logs/human-annotation-trace-details.png)

### Using saved views for annotation queues

To make specific views based on filters for raters to annotate, you can use saved views. This allows you to create filtered queues of logs that need annotation. Raters can:

1. Apply filters to narrow down logs that need annotation (e.g., unannotated logs, specific time ranges, or certain criteria)

2. Save these filtered views for quick access

3. Use saved views to work through annotation queues systematically

This helps organize annotation work and ensures raters focus on the most relevant logs for evaluation.

## Viewing annotations

Annotations can be viewed in two places:

### Logs table

Human evaluator scores are displayed as columns in the logs table. The scores shown are the average across all team members who have annotated that log. Click on any score cell to add or edit your annotation.

### Trace details (Evaluation tab)

When you open a trace, the `Evaluation` tab shows:

- **Average scores**: Aggregated scores for each human evaluator

- **Individual annotations**: Breakdown of scores, comments, and rewritten outputs from each team member

- **Pass/fail status**: Whether the log passed or failed based on evaluator criteria

## Understanding annotation scores

- **Average scores**: When multiple team members annotate the same log, the average score is shown in the table columns

- **Individual breakdown**: Click on any annotation to see individual scores, comments, and rewritten outputs from each annotator

- **Pass/fail**: Scores are evaluated against pass/fail criteria defined in the evaluator configuration

- **Rewritten outputs**: Multiple team members can provide rewritten outputs; all versions are visible in the trace details view

Human annotation helps improve your AI applications by providing qualitative feedback that automated systems cannot capture, enabling continuous refinement based on real-world usage.
