## Pricing Comparison

| Metric | Maxim | Comet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Tier | ✅ Up to 10k requests (Logs & Traces) | ✅ Up to 25k spans per month & 60-day data retention |

Maxim offers more affordable and predictable usage-based pricing at $1/10k logs. In contrast, retention costs for span-based plans like Comet’s may grow unpredictably at scale. Additionally, Maxim’s seat-based pricing provides better access control for teams, whereas Comet’s unlimited-user model lacks that granularity, making Maxim more suitable for structured, collaborative enterprise setups.

## Conclusion

If your organization needs structured agent simulations, secure evaluation workflows, and fine-grained observability at production scale, Maxim is the clear choice. It supports everything from tool-using agents to secure human-in-the-loop evaluations with enterprise controls.

Comet (Opik) is a solid observability platform for prompt logging, LLM tracking, and experiment comparison. But it lacks the simulation depth and compliance rigor that Maxim offers, making it more of a tracking tool than an end-to-end AgentOps platform.

Maxim for scale, compliance, and full-stack agent observability. Comet for lightweight prompt tracking and experiments.
