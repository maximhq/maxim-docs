---
title: SNR (Signal-To-Noise Ratio)
description: Calculates a score based on the ratio between the desired signal power and background noise power in the audio.
---
<Note>SNR is a Statistical Metric</Note>
## Input
- **Required Inputs:**
  - **`actual_output`**: URL of the audio recording to evaluate

## Output
- **`Result`**: Numeric SNR value (e.g., in dB)
- **`Reasoning`**: Brief explanation of audio quality assessment


## Interpretation
- **Higher values**: Clearer audio with less background noise
- **Lower values**: Significant noise interference impacting clarity

> SNR of 20dB indicates _**Good**_ quality, while 5dB indicates _**Very Bad**_ quality
