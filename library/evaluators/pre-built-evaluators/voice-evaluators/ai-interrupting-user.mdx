---
title: AI Interrupting User
description: Identifies and counts the number of instances where the AI interrupts or cuts off user speech during conversations.
---

## Input
- **Required Inputs:**
  - **`actual_output`**: URL of the audio recording to evaluate

## Output
- **`Result`**: Count of inappropriate interruptions (integer)
- **`Reasoning`**: Brief explanation of detected interruptions

## Interpretation
- **Higher counts**: More frequent AI interruptions 
- **Lower counts**: Better turn-taking and conversational etiquette
