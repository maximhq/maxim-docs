---
title: Sentiment Analysis
description: Analyzes the speech patterns, acoustic features like pitch and vocal tone along with the conversational content of an pre recorded audio conversation to deliver precise sentiment classification
---

## Input
- **Required Inputs:**
  - **`actual_output`**: URL of the audio recording to evaluate

## Output
- **`Result`**: One of `positive`, `negative`, or `neutral`
- **`Reasoning`**: Brief explanation for the classification

## Interpretation
- **positive**: Predominantly positive affect (e.g., satisfied, pleased)
- **negative**: Predominantly negative affect (e.g., angry, frustrated)
- **neutral**: Largely neutral, very casual without any emotions shown
