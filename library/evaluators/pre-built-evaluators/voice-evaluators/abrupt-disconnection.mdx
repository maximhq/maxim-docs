---
title: Abrupt Disconnection
description: Detects if there is unexpected call terminations or early termination at the end of a pre recorded audio recording 
---

## Input
- **Required Inputs:**
  - **`output`**: URL of the audio recording to evaluate

## Output
- **`Result`**: Binary score (0 or 1)
- **`Reasoning`**: Brief explanation of the detection

<Note>
  Supported models: Gemini 2.5 Flash (recommended), GPT-4o Audio Preview, Gemini 2.5 Pro. Gemini 2.5 Flash is recommended for best performance and latency.
</Note>

## Interpretation
- **1**: Abrupt disconnection detected
- **0**: No abrupt disconnection detected
