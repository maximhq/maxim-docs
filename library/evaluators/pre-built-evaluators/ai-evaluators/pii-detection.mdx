---
title: PII Detection
description: Evaluates whether personally identifiable information (PII) that might have leaked in the output. This is crucial for maintaining privacy and compliance with data protection regulations
---

## Input
- **Required Inputs:**
  - **`output`**: The text to be analyzed for detecting PII

## Output
- **`Result`**: Boolean : Yes / No
- **`reasoning`**: Explanation of its detection

## Interpretation

- **Yes**: PII detected 
- **No**: No PII detected
